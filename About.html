ABOUT MEKONG PACK SOLUTIONS

Practical supply.
Human connection.

Mekong Pack Solutions is a simulated Cambodian packaging supplier based in Phnom Penh. The company has operated since 2018.

This student website introduces paper bags, cardboard boxes, food containers and protective packaging to retail customers, businesses and potential partners.

A clear approach to packaging

A useful supplier website helps people understand the range and ask the right questions. These principles guide how we communicate.

Be practical

Organize products by use and explain the basics customers should confirm.

Be clear

Use straightforward language. Keep product and enquiry information easy to find.

Be approachable

Make room for real questions, business needs and partnership discussions.

Who we work with

Retail customers

Small & medium businesses

Teams planning packaging for products, storage, takeaway and dispatch.

Suppliers & partners

Businesses exploring product supply and potential collaboration.
